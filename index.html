Audio: off. Tap Play to start.

Loop player: breaks speed up or slow down to follow the tempo, which also shifts their pitch, like an MPC or SP-1200. Use loop tempo sets 4TRADE to the break's own speed. If a loop sounds twice as fast or slow, change its Length.

Field recorder: with the groove, recording starts with the count-in and stops when you stop the groove. With groove plays a take back in time with the drums. If your playing sounds late or early against the groove, move Sync fix until it lines up. It's set once per device.

Kit and Loop player each have their own import and library. Import the whole pack into both: the Kit keeps the one-shots, the Loop player keeps the breaks. Use ◀ ▶ to step through sounds while the groove plays, or tap the sound name to browse and search. Favorites save the kit too. Samples are stored in this browser on this device only.

New pattern clears both the groove and the solo bed and keeps your tempo, kit and loop. Program it, name it, then Save to favorites and tap a slot.

Favorites: tap a slot to load it. While playing, it switches at the top of the next bar. To save what you have, tap Store, then a slot.

Variation follows the phrase: bar 1 plays as written, bar 2 brings a variation that comes back in bar 4, and the last bar fills into the next section. The clave, the kick on 1 and the backbeats never move. While playing, a dot marks an added hit and a faded pad marks one left out.

Tap a pad once for a hit, twice for a ghost note (outlined, quiet), three times to clear. Edit either pattern with the Groove / Solo bed tabs. Presets load both. Humanize pushes hats early, drags snare and clap late, sits the kick back, then adds small random timing and volume drift. Tap an instrument name to hear it, M to mute. Your patterns stay on this device.
